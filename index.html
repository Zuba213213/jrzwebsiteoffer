Questions

Straight answers.

Do I really own the website?

Yes. Your domain, hosting account and every login are set up in your name from day one. We work inside accounts you own. If we ever stop working together, you keep everything and owe us nothing to get it.

How long does it take?

You'll usually see the first draft of your site and your Marketing Vault within a few days of our interview. We review it together in week one, make your changes, and launch within 30 days of kickoff. The date goes in your contract. If we miss the date for reasons on our side, you get 3 months of site care free.

What do I need to provide?

About 45 minutes for an interview, access to your existing accounts (if any), and any photos you'd like used. We write all the words. You review and approve.

Is the free mockup really free?

Yes. We design your new homepage so you can see the direction before deciding anything. If you don't move forward, no charge and no follow-up spam.

Why is it $2,500 when the parts add up to $6,000?

It's founding-client pricing. We're building our portfolio of service-business websites, and early clients get the full website and the Vault research for the price of one.

What are the ongoing costs?

You pay your own domain and hosting directly, usually a small yearly or monthly fee depending on the provider. An optional care plan covers updates, backups and edits after launch.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes. Redesigns are welcome. We'll keep what's working, move your domain over safely, and rebuild the rest around your customer research.
