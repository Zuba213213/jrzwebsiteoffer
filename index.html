Questions

Straight answers.

Who builds my site, and where are you?

I do. JRZ is me, Joey Zubillaga. I lived and worked in the DFW area and now work remotely with clients across the US. You deal with me directly from the first call to launch, by text, WhatsApp, phone or video call.

Do I really own the website?

Yes. Your domain, hosting account and every login are set up in your name from day one, and I work inside accounts you own. If we ever stop working together, you keep everything and owe nothing to get it.

How long does it take?

You'll usually see the first drafts of your site and your Marketing Vault within a few days of our interview. We review them together in week one, I make your changes, and your site launches within 30 days of kickoff. The date goes in your contract.

What if I don't like the first drafts?

You get two rounds of revisions before anything goes live. If you decide not to continue after seeing the drafts, you can stop there: you keep the drafts and the research, and you don't owe the remaining $1,500.

Will this get me found on Google Maps?

Your website is one part of how Google ranks you. I set up the parts the site controls (page titles, a page for each service and city you serve, fast mobile load) and connect it to your Google Business Profile with a link that makes it easy for customers to leave reviews. Reviews and your Google profile matter too, and I'll show you how to keep both strong. Ranking itself isn't something anyone can honestly guarantee.

What are the ongoing costs?

You pay your domain and hosting directly, in your name, usually a small yearly or monthly fee depending on the provider. After launch, an optional month-to-month care plan covers updates, backups and edits, with no long-term contract.

Can I make changes to the site myself?

Tell me how hands-on you want to be before we start. If you or your office want to edit pages yourselves, I'll build it on a platform you can log into and update. If you'd rather text me the change, the care plan covers it.

Why is it only $2,500?

It's founding-client pricing. I'm building my portfolio of DFW service-business websites, so early clients get the full website and the Vault research for the price of the Vault alone.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes. I'll keep what's working, move your domain over safely, and rebuild the rest around your customer research. Lost access to your old site? Tell me in the form, and I'll help you work through getting it back.
